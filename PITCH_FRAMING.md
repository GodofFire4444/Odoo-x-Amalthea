# 💡 Pitch Framing: Problem, Solution & Why It Matters

## 1. The Problem: Why Expense Management is Broken

### Customer Pain Points

**For Employees:**
- Manual receipt scanning and data entry = 30+ minutes per expense
- Lost receipts = rejected reimbursements
- Confusion about approval status = constant check-ins
- Waiting days/weeks for approval = cash flow pressure
- Different processes at different companies = learning curve

**For Managers:**
- Email-based approvals scattered across inbox
- No visibility into bottlenecks or compliance
- Manual state management = inconsistent decisions
- No audit trail = policy enforcement fails
- Multi-currency approval headaches

**For Finance Teams:**
- Impossible to scale workflows manually
- Duplicate submissions slip through
- Multi-location compliance is a nightmare
- Integration with accounting software is manual
- No real-time visibility into spend

### Market Reality
- Expense management is a **$3B+ market** globally
- Companies waste **$8,000–$15,000 per employee annually** on unmanaged travel/expenses
- Approval delays cost businesses **5–7% of claimed expenses**
- Manual processes introduce **15–20% error rate**

### Why Current Solutions Fail
- **Legacy software** (Concur, Expensify): Expensive, complicated, inflexible
- **Spreadsheets**: Don't scale, no audit trail, no automation
- **Custom in-house tools**: Expensive to build, maintain, extend
- **Missing global support**: Single-currency, single-approval-style solutions

## 2. The Solution: Amalthea Expense Management

### Core Value Proposition
**"Intelligent Expense Management. Flexible Workflows. Real-Time Visibility."**

Amalthea is a modern SaaS platform that automates expense submission, approval, and tracking with an intelligent workflow engine that adapts to any company structure.

### Key Differentiators

#### 🎯 **Flexible Workflow Engine** (What Makes Us Unique)
- **Sequential Rules**: Expenses route through defined approvers in order
- **Percentage Rules**: Approval thresholds (e.g., "2 out of 3 managers must approve")
- **Specific Approver Rules**: CEO must approve all meals
- **Hybrid Rules**: Complex combinations (sequential + threshold)
- **No Code Required**: Admins configure rules in minutes, no engineering involvement

**Why It Matters:**
- Startups approve via CEO
- Mid-market needs multi-level hierarchies
- Enterprise needs complex compliance rules
- **One platform scales to all.** No vendor lock-in.

#### 📸 **Smart OCR Technology**
- Scans receipts → extracts vendor, amount, date, category automatically
- Reduces manual data entry from 10 minutes to 30 seconds
- Learns over time (with ML integration potential)
- Works offline, processes in background

**Why It Matters:**
- Friction = users won't use it. OCR removes friction.
- Employees get instant feedback, don't wait for processing.

#### 💱 **Multi-Currency Support**
- Expense in any currency, approves in base currency
- Real-time exchange rates integrated
- Compliance-aware (tracks spends by location/country)
- Global scale out of the box

**Why It Matters:**
- International teams demand this
- Legacy solutions charge premium for "multi-currency"
- Baked into our DNA from Day 1

#### 📊 **Real-Time Insights & Audit Trail**
- Admins see all expenses, approvals, compliance status instantly
- Complete audit trail (who, what, when, why for every decision)
- Spending trends by department, location, category
- Integration-ready (APIs for accounting, HR platforms)

**Why It Matters:**
- Compliance is non-negotiable
- Finance teams need real-time spend visibility
- No more "where did that $50K go?" questions

#### ⚡ **Mobile-First Design**
- Approve on the go
- Employees can submit from mobile camera
- Responsive design, works on any device
- Ready for iOS/Android apps (Phase 2)
